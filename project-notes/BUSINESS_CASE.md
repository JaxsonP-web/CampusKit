## Business case technology  

 

## the idea  

I came up with the idea because i thought it would save a lot of people money, buying equipment or gear you may not need for a long time can be expensive and not worth its value since you're not using it for long. Or if you lost one part of your equipment and you can’t play because of it weather it’s a guitar, hockey stick, chord, gloves or helmet whatever it may instead of buying a new one you could use this alternative method. Especially all of the extra gear we would have from all of our sport teams at StFX. I would have StFX student life pay an annual fee to run CampusKit so some students could borrow equipment for free. Student life we would have a reason to pay for this because it would make more students join clubs and easier for them to. Without having to buy all the new stuff for it. For example, A student who needs a guitar for one campus event could request one to pick up which would make borrowing easier to organize.  

 

## Where it creates value  

CampusKit makes it easier for students to find equipment on campus right now and it's effective and reliable. If students need a guitar for an event, they might text their friends or who they think might have one. Even if they find a club with a guitar, they must wait for an answer to see if it's available or if they would even let them borrow it. The filter in my prototype shows how the student could narrow down a shared list of equipment to find their needs. Clubs could all update their listings where gear is borrowed or returned. So, they could check availability before submitting their request. CampusKit would also make it easier for clubs to manage borrowing requests. If a request starts in a group chat or a direct message, it’s easy to miss or lose of who asked for gear or what time they arranged. My prototype uses a request form to collect those details in one place. The clubs could approve and confirm the pickup window and time. The students could then see if their request is still pending or if it went through and got approved. The club would then have a record to check when the comes to pick it up. 

 

## Network effects 

Both sides benefit from Campuskit. When More campus groups listing gear gives students more choices. More successful loans and on time returns give groups a reason to keep listing their gear. For example, if one club’s guitar is already borrowed, a student might find another one listed by a different group. The school then benefits through getting more people involved in everything in all the clubs and teams. Clubs also have a reason to keep listing gear when they see students using the equipment that would just sit around and not be used. If pickups are easy to organize and students return items on time, clubs may feel more comfortable listing more gear. This could show how easy it could be to help others and make people join new things they typically wouldn't join but something they always wanted to try. I used the Pilot and Expanded settings in my prototype to simulate what CampusKit could look like with different numbers of listings. In the Pilot setting when I looked for “item or category you tested” I saw what was available. In the expanded setting, I saw “what changed”. This showed how adding more listings could explain what improved the student. Such as having a backup option or finding a type of gear that was missing before. To get CampusKit started here at StFX I would ask a student music group or multiple student groups and a club that runs campus events to list a few items they would be comfortable and willing to lend. These could be guitars, music stands, or speakers. Once those items were listed. I’d ask the groups to share the CampusKit link wherever they would want to weather that's online or posters or just in their group chats before an upcoming event. That would give students a reason to try it out when they actually need something. I would then use their feedback to see what changes I would need to make. 

 

## Can it be copied? 

I think another team could copy the basic idea behind CampusKit. A searchable equipment list. The category filters and a request form are all things someone else could build. If another student team made a similar website, students could use it to look for equipment in the same way. CampusKit would have a stronger advantage if clubs were already comfortable lending through it and students knew that they could count and rely on the return process.  

The two main things I would focus on building at StFX Would be first. Relationships with campus groups that lend equipment. These relationships would be valuable because clubs decide what gear they are willing to share, when they need it back, and who can approve requests. For example, a group may be willing to let you borrow a spare guitar once it has had a few successful loans and knows how to contact the borrower if it comes back late. A competitor could contact the same group and offer to list its equipment too. That means relationships would only come harder over time as CampusKit earns all these clubs and teams to trust. A competitor could also start with different clubs and build its own equipment selection. I would try to keep these clubs involved by asking what they would be comfortable lending rather than asking for specific items. 

The second resource would be A reliable process for pickups and returns. This would be valuable because lending can create extra work for club volunteers. For example, if a student borrows a speaker for an event, the club needs to know who has it, when it's due back, and whether it will be available for the next upcoming event. A competitor could copy features like reminders and request status; it could also use a shared Calander and offer in person pickup. CampusKit could build an advantage by learning from actual loans and figuring out what works for each club. We could even eventually go to making potential deliveries to use that as a competive advantage over other competitors. At this very stage, these are advantages CampusKit could build. I would start with a few clubs; track weather items are picked up and returned as agreed upon. And ask if they would lend through us again. If they keep lending, we can keep on expanding by coming up with more ideas. 

 

## Cloud and Operations 

To turn my CampusKit prototype into a service students could use, I would host the website online and connect it to a shared database. The database would store the equipment listings, borrowing requests, pickup times, and return dates. Clubs could update their listings from their own devices and could see whether the item they are looking for is available or not before requesting it. When a student requests a guitar, the music group will receive the request, check whether the guitar is available, and approve a pickup time. Once their request is approved, the guitar will be marked as reserved for that period of time. That is for another student who couldn’t book it at the same time and prevent double bookings. CampusKit could also send reminders to you before the pickup and another before the return deadline. 

 

## Risks and ethics 

One risk is collecting more student information than CampusKit needs. I would use a student's name and stfx email to manage the loan and let the lending club contact them about either the pickup or return. Only the people actually handling the request should be able to see those details. Students should also be told what information is collected and how long it will be kept. Another risk is that equipment could be returned late, damaged or lost. If a guitar is due back before the clubs' next event, a late return could affect the whole group. I would show the return date before the student actually confirms the request so they can make sure they can meet that deadline. And also have the club check the items conditions before then Aswell as pick up to see if it got damaged over the time they had it.
